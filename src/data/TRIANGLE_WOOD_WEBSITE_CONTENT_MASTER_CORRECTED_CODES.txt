1. Corrected product-code convention
The product codes now include the literal "TW" followed by the four-digit colour code, then the three-letter colour suffix. Apply this format consistently to every colour and pattern.

Construction note: engineered wood flooring with a genuine hardwood wear layer. Highlight the proprietary TRIANGLE WOOD Click System(tm) for Wide Plank and Herringbone; the Chevron profile is listed with a precision tongue-and-groove connection in the prior website content.
Wear layer: 3-4 mm genuine hardwood, subject to SKU/factory confirmation. UV surface treatment: UV-cured protective treatment; the exact coating system must be confirmed by the factory. All sixteen colours are stained finishes, including colours that appear natural or pale.
Installation pattern philosophy
* Khufu Horizon - Wide Plank: continuity, openness and the uninterrupted reading of wood grain.
* Osiris Rhythm - Herringbone 90°: cadence, craftsmanship and geometric elegance.
* Horus Vector - Chevron 45°: direction, perspective and architectural movement.
